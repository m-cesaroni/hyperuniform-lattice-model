A discrete, random, equilibrium vacuum: what it reproduces, what it predicts, what it cannot yet do

Working note — M. Cesaroni, October 2026. Model and numerical checks; no claim of completeness. Italian original: nota-tecnica.html. Terms are defined in the glossary.

In three sentences. Space is modelled as a three-dimensional random lattice of cells in mechanical equilibrium; light, matter and gravity are ways of using the faces, edges and vertices of the Voronoi tessellation. On that lattice the electromagnetic field (discrete exterior calculus) and a Kähler–Dirac field come out right without tuning: exact c, isotropy, no birefringence, no long-wavelength scattering, the relativistic energy–momentum relation as an algebraic identity. Time is not a coordinate of the model: it is the count of chirality inversions of a massive vibration, and time dilation, special and gravitational, is bookkeeping. From the same structure come the factor 2 in light deflection, Mercury's perihelion from the saturation of a probabilistic occupation, and three generations from the geometry of channels; the model predicts a constant dark energy and a black-hole shadow 4.6 % larger than in general relativity, and dies if the data say otherwise.

1. The model, in standard terms

Three choices, all different from the known discrete-spacetime programmes (causal sets, dynamical triangulations, spin networks, cellular automata):

  1. Random lattice, not crystalline. Points with a minimum distance, fixed density, periodic boundaries. No preferred axes; rotational invariance is statistical.
  2. Mechanical equilibrium. The points repel each other with a finite-range potential and are relaxed until the forces vanish. The resulting lattice is hyperuniform (S(k) → 0 as k → 0, fluctuations ∝ surface) and locally isotropic in the directions of its neighbours. This condition is what makes everything else work.
  3. The Delaunay–Voronoi duality as the home of the fields. 0-forms on cells, 1-forms on links (dual to Voronoi faces), 2-forms on triangles (dual to edges), 3-forms on tetrahedra (dual to vertices). Diagonal Hodge stars are ratios of dual measures. The EM field lives on the 1- and 2-forms; the spinor on all four (Kähler–Dirac).

Time evolution: Hermitian Hamiltonian K = d + d† (symmetrised with the Hodge stars); for light, an energy-exact leapfrog. Units: mean lattice step = 1, c = 1 in the continuum.

Matrix mechanics. The state is a vector of amplitudes on the elements of the complex; the position X_a is diagonal (coordinate of the dual centre of each element: cell, link midpoint, triangle centre, tetrahedron centre); K is sparse, with entries only between adjacent elements. For any diagonal matrix ([X_a, K])_{αβ} = (x_α − x_β)_a K_{αβ}, real and antisymmetric, hence [X_a, K] = iV_a with V_a Hermitian: Heisenberg's equation dX_a/dt = i[K, X_a] = V_a identifies V_a with the velocity (displacement along the passage × stiffness of the passage). Robertson's inequality follows: ΔX_a·ΔK ≥ ½|⟨V_a⟩|. In the Schrödinger limit [X_a, P_a] = i in lattice units (cell × radian per cell): ħ is a unit conversion. The uncertainty principle is verified from the spectrum of K (table), saturating the Gaussian minimum above one cell and reduced below it (minimal length).

2. Time

There is no time coordinate in the model. The lattice updates in steps — batches — but a step is not an instant: it is a global counter that no observer can read. Physical time is something else, and it is defined operationally.

  • Proper time is a count. A massive vibration inverts its chirality at frequency 2E/ħ (Zitterbewegung); the mass operator is what inverts. The proper time of a particle is the number of inversions it has completed. On the lattice, for every eigenstate, ⟨Γ⟩ = m/E, and the number of inversions per step is the share T of the budget, with I² + T² = 1 exact. T = dτ/dt = 1/γ follows without postulating relativity; at discrete steps the relation becomes cos ω = cos μ · cos κ and tends to the continuous one for small angles.
  • Light has no time. The EM field lives on the 1- and 2-forms and has no inversion term: no count, τ = 0, consistent with relativity. This is also why light does not interact with itself and is not localisable: nothing happens to it between emission and absorption.
  • Gravitational dilation is a rhythm. Near a mass the occupation n slows the inversions: T² = e^(−n), whence redshift, GPS, Shapiro, and — with the same deficit read in space as well — γ = 1. The binding energy of hydrogen in Dirac's theory is exactly the deficit of the electron's proper time: ⟨β⟩ = √(1 − α²), E = mT.
  • Before the first vibration there is no time. A lattice without massive excitations has steps but no clocks. The initial growth epoch has a number of steps, not a duration: "how long did inflation last" is not a well-posed question in the model, and a birth rate per step in that epoch is not a physical quantity. Time begins with the first excitation, i.e. with the first push on a closed shell of equilibrium cells.
  • The twin paradox and the energy–time uncertainty are counts: the travelling twin spent more budget in motion and less in inversions; knowing E to precision ΔE requires ΔE·Δt ≳ ħ because it requires counting enough steps.

Verified constraint: the conversion between steps and the proper time of a cosmic observer does not drift with the epoch — a fit to the DESI DR2 BAO distances excludes a clock factor depending on Ω_m (parameter ≈ 0 ± 0.1). The model's time does not rewrite cosmological chronology. Experimental reference for the time–mass identification: Lan et al., Science 339 (2013), the Compton clock.

4. What it reproduces, and with how many assumptions

With no free parameter

  • Relativistic energy–momentum relation and the budget v² + (1/γ)² = 1 as consequences of the anticommutation motion–inversion; proper time as the count of inversions.
  • The hydrogen spectrum in the Rydberg scale, the accidental degeneracies and the intensities of Lyman-α and Balmer 2p→3d from the lattice Schrödinger equation, without tuned constants; the cubic splitting of the 3d states belongs to the periodic images and disappears under a spherical wall.
  • Zitterbewegung frequency 2E/ħ, oscillation along the spin axis, amplitude c/2m.
  • Deflection 1.7516″, Shapiro 123.7 μs, GPS +38.5 μs/day from the principle "one deficit read in time and in space" (γ = 1).
  • Mercury 42.99″/century from the saturation of the occupation (β = 1), with no added term.
  • Three generations, no fourth: three faces around a Voronoi edge; Koide's relation Q = 2/3 as an identity of the three projections at 120°.
  • No magnetic monopoles (d² = 0) and no light–light interaction (linearity), by structure.

With one parameter or one identification

  • Hierarchy 1 : 207 : 3477 if the dipole angle is the typical imbalance of the channels, which comes out 0.21–0.23 rad at packing fraction 0.70 (0.28 at 0.60; 0.17–0.21 at 0.80).
  • Neutrino masses 0.38 / 8.9 / 50.7 meV (Brannen's scheme): two parameters, reproduces both Δm².
  • G equivalent to 5–6 "engagements per inversion" with the cell at the Planck scale; ≈ half of the 11.7 neighbours that fix α = 1/N².
  • Uniform chirality of the universe from the first birth-ordered tetrahedron (sign); the amount of the baryon asymmetry remains to be derived.

5. The known obstacles, by name

  • Lorentz invariance. A preferred frame exists (the lattice). It is statistically invisible: corrections are quadratic, E_QG,2 ≈ 2.7 E_Planck against current limits ≈ 10⁻⁸ E_Planck; the time of flight of GRB 090510 comes out 2·10⁻¹⁹ s.
  • Fermion doubling. A spinor placed in the cells produces a constant density of spurious modes (p = 2 in the scattering: conversion into lattice modes). Kähler–Dirac removes them at the price of two nearly degenerate tastes (0.1–0.3 % splitting at λ = 15 steps). Not solved: the main open problem on matter.
  • Aether. It is Lorentz's aether made invisible by hyperuniformity: real parts, undetectable by waves longer than the cells. Where it differs from GR it is measurable (below).
  • Speed of the gravitational contagion. The 1/r profile was obtained with a random walk, incompatible with GW170817 by 30 orders of magnitude. The profile survives as the static solution of a wave equation; the mechanism must be redone.
  • Vacuum energy and masses. The model does not solve the hierarchy: it translates it into "why inversion is rare" (m/m_P per step). The 120 orders of magnitude of the vacuum energy remain.

7. What it cannot do (today)

  1. Separate or interpret the two tastes of the Kähler–Dirac field.
  2. Construct the "cell–void exchange" mass operator that anticommutes with K (the naive version transports instead of inverting).
  3. Fix the packing fraction of the vacuum: Koide's angle is a one-parameter coincidence until φ is derived.
  4. The growth of structure with this gravity (needed to compare the 60 meV with lensing limits).
  5. The weak sector (W, Z, coupling to one chirality) and the amount of the baryon asymmetry.

8. Reproducibility

All numbers in the table come from Python scripts (numpy, scipy) that run on a laptop: lattice generation (RSA, equilibrium, growth by adjacent births, CCC crystal as control), measurements of S(k) and number variance, light propagation with discrete exterior calculus (leapfrog, energy conserved to 10⁻¹²), Weyl and Kähler–Dirac operators with exact diagonalisation on small lattices, Monte Carlo contagion. Times: 30 minutes for 3·10⁴ cells, an hour and a half for 1.3·10⁵. The scripts are in the repository's code/ folder (mt_trasparenza.py, mt_luce.py, kdlib.py, mt_idrogeno_pc.py), and in code/analysis/ those that produce the individual numbers of the table (channel geometry, neighbours, uncertainty, commutator, sectors, pushes, DESI fit). The gravity numbers — 1/r profile from the contagion, Mercury from saturation — come from scripts of an earlier phase not yet consolidated in the repository: they will be added in a later version.
